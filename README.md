--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stop wrestling with complicated spreadsheets and generic templates. ProInvoice
is an all-in-one mobile billing and invoicing powerhouse designed to help
freelancers, consultants, contractors, agencies, and small business owners get
paid faster and look more professional.

Whether you are generating a quick service bill on-site, managing multiple
business entities, or sending a statutory GST/Tax invoice to an enterprise
client, ProInvoice turns complex billing into a seamless 60-second workflow.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY FEATURES & CAPABILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. 8 DESIGNER PDF INVOICE TEMPLATES & LIVE PREVIEW
Stand out in your client's inbox with eight handcrafted, high-resolution vector
PDF layouts that adapt dynamically to your brand:
  * Modern Slate     : Clean geometric cards, crisp pill headers & floating totals.
  * Corporate Pro    : Executive navy header with structured dual address blocks.
  * Luxe Atelier     : Warm champagne gold accents & refined editorial styling.
  * Nordic Minimal   : Generous negative space with soft teal borderless rows.
  * Bauhaus Neo      : High-contrast impact banners and bold summary highlights.
  * Pastel Studio    : Warm creative studio aesthetic with rounded container cards.
  * Fintech Receipt  : Direct-pay aesthetic with compact tabular line items.
  * Formal GST Tax   : Statutory tax layout with dedicated Rate/Taxable columns
                       and authorized signatory blocks.

2. MULTI-BUSINESS PROFILE & BRANDING HUB
Run more than one venture? Switch between business identities effortlessly:
  * Save multiple sender ("From") business profiles with full contact details,
    tax IDs, addresses, and default payment instructions.
  * Upload and embed your custom Company Logo directly onto your invoices.
  * Set a default business profile to pre-fill new invoices automatically.

3. SMART CLIENT & REUSABLE ITEM CATALOG
Never type the same product or client twice:
  * Item & Service Catalog: Store your products, hourly rates, SKU codes, and
    descriptions. Insert catalog items into any invoice with a single tap.
  * Client Directory: Save customer billing addresses, emails, and phone numbers
    for instant auto-completion on future bills.

4. PRECISION TAX, GST & DISCOUNT ENGINE
Eliminate manual math errors with real-time financial calculations:
  * Flexible Discounts: Apply Percentage (%) or Flat-rate discounts with
    automatic taxable value recalculation per line item.
  * Custom Tax Rates: Configure GST, VAT, or Sales Tax percentages that compute
    instantaneously on net taxable subtotals.
  * Multi-Currency Support: Bill local or international clients in your
    preferred currency symbol ($, €, £, ₹, ¥, and more).

5. REAL-TIME AUTO-SAVE & DRAFT RECOVERY
Never lose a single keystroke:
  * Intelligent background auto-save continuously backs up your active invoice
    as you type, displaying live save timestamps ("Saved just now").
  * Seamlessly resume unfinished drafts at any time.

6. COMPLETE PAYMENT TERMS, BANK DETAILS & NOTES
Ensure clients have everything they need to pay you on time:
  * Dedicated Notes & Payment Terms block with intelligent multi-line word
    wrapping for bank account numbers, IBAN/SWIFT codes, UPI IDs, wire
    instructions, and warranty policies.
  * Clean, distraction-free PDF output with zero third-party watermarks.

7. REVENUE ANALYTICS & INVOICE TRACKING DASHBOARD
Stay on top of your cash flow at a glance:
  * Track invoice lifecycles across four clear statuses:
    Draft, Sent, Paid, and Overdue.
  * View real-time financial summaries of Total Billed, Paid Revenue, and
    Outstanding Receivables.
  * Filter and search invoices instantly by client name, invoice number, or
    status badge.

8. ONE-TAP PDF EXPORT, SHARE & WIRELESS PRINTING
Deliver invoices how your clients prefer:
  * Generate crisp, print-ready A4 PDF documents natively on your device.
  * Share directly via Email, WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Drive, or any
    installed sharing app.
  * Print directly to Wi-Fi and Bluetooth printers using native Android print
    integration.

9. PRIVACY-FIRST & 100% OFFLINE READY
Your financial data belongs to you:
  * Powered by an ultra-fast local database—create, edit, search, and generate
    PDFs anywhere, even without an internet connection.
  * Built-in Admin Management Hub to inspect, maintain, and organize all stored
    records with ease.
